The verdict

“One of the best trips we've ever had. Everything is unbelievably green, the sausages are perfect, the beers are fun, and the kids are in a dream.” Shelly, after her family's stay

Drawback 1: remote

Many locals don't speak English. Google Translate gets you through.

Drawback 2: cold

Dress really warm. They had cold weather but not a single day of rain.

Shelly's top tip

Rent a golf cart.

The park is huge and April is cold. A golf cart upgrades everything and makes getting around the grounds effortless.

Central building

It has everything. Restaurants are OK: they ordered a few times but cooked in the cottage a lot.

The bakery

Excellent. They topped up the morning pastries daily and picked up treats whenever needed.

Groceries

A very well stocked shop on site, plus Lidl supermarkets a short drive away.

Day trips

Into the mountains

Alpsee Bergwelt · 40 min

Germany's longest summer toboggan run, plus the AlpseeSkytrail ropes park next door. Kids ride on a parent's lap.

Skywalk Allgäu · 45 min

Treetop walkway on 4 levels, stroller friendly, with a lift. Playground, water play, barefoot trail and animal corner at the entrance.

Day trips

Theme parks

Legoland Deutschland · ~1 hr

Rides and activities from age one, ideal up to about 10. Buy skip-the-line tickets in advance.

Ravensburger Spieleland · 40 min

Closer, and especially good for little kids.

Skyline Park · 45 min

60 rides, mostly for older kids, some for small ones.

Day trips

Lake Constance, a full day

About an hour away, where Germany, Austria and Switzerland meet. Nature and town in one day.

Before we go

Practical tips

Comfortable car

We drive most days and the kids will nap on the way. Worth upgrading.

Book the golf cart

Reserve it with the cottage so it's there from day one.

Pack for cold

Warm coats, hats and gloves, even in late April.

Offline German

Download Google Translate German. English is limited locally.

Cook in

Use the cottage kitchen, the on-site shop and the nearby Lidl.

Check April dates

Confirm seasonal openings for Legoland, Spieleland, toboggans and cable cars.
